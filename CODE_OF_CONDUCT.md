# Code of Conduct

pkgguard aims to be a welcoming project for everyone who wants to make
software supply chains safer, whatever their background or experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith, especially in bug reports and reviews.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that seem basic.
- Respect the privacy of others, including in security reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information without consent.
- Using the project's issue tracker to share live malware, stolen
  credentials, or instructions for attacking specific people or
  organizations.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that
violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently block contributors who
repeatedly or severely violate it.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@KhanSaahib](https://github.com/KhanSaahib),
via the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled
confidentially.

This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
